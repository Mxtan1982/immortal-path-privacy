Privacy Policy

Immortal Path: Defy Fate

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Immortal Path: Defy Fate handles information when you use the mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not require users to create an account and does not directly ask users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

However, the app uses third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. These services may automatically collect or process certain device and usage information, which may include:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Device type, operating system, language, and app version
  • Advertising interaction data, diagnostics, and performance information

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party advertising services may be used to:

  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Improve advertising performance and service reliability
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google may process data according to its own privacy policies and advertising technologies.

4. Advertising

This app contains advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Rewarded ads are optional and are shown only after a user chooses to watch them.

Depending on the user's region, consent or privacy choices may be presented before personalized advertising is used.

5. Local App Data

The app may save reading progress, bookmarks, theme preferences, font size, and language preferences locally on the user's device. This local data is used only to provide app functionality and is not intentionally uploaded by the app to our own servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by third-party service providers such as Google AdMob as necessary to provide advertising and related services.

7. Data Retention

Reading preferences stored locally remain on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the app. Data processed by third-party services is subject to the retention practices of those providers.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration or user-submitted forms.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize unnecessary data collection and use secure third-party services where applicable. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. International Users

The app may be available in multiple countries. Third-party services may process information in countries other than the user's country of residence, subject to applicable law and their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the developer contact details listed on the Google Play Store page for this app.
